Who We Are

Ambassadors of Encouragement International Ministries (A.E.M.I) is a Spirit-filled, Bible-believing community of faith based in Kampala, Uganda. We are a family of believers united by a passionate relationship with Jesus Christ and a deep love for people.

Our name reflects our calling — we are ambassadors sent by God to encourage, equip, and empower individuals, families, and communities through the transforming power of the Gospel.

We believe that the Church is God's instrument for changing the world, and every member has a vital role to play in this great mission.

Our Story

Our History

From humble beginnings to a thriving international ministry, A.E.M.I's story is one of God's faithfulness, visionary leadership, and a community committed to the Great Commission.

Over the decades, God has expanded our borders — from a small gathering in Kampala to a ministry that is touching lives across Uganda and beyond. The Ambassadors of Encouragement Church Komamboga stands today as a testament to what God can do through a people fully surrendered to Him.

"What God started, He will complete. A.E.M.I exists because of His faithfulness across generations."

1990s

The Beginning

A.E.M.I was founded with a small group of believers passionate about encouraging one another and proclaiming the Gospel in Kampala.

2000s

Growth & Expansion

The ministry grew rapidly as more people encountered God's love. Cell groups were established across the city, creating a strong discipleship network.

2010s

International Reach

The "International" vision came to fruition as A.E.M.I began reaching beyond Uganda, sending missionaries and establishing partnerships across Africa.

2015

Media & Technology

Launch of online broadcasts and digital media ministry, enabling the Gospel to reach believers and seekers around the world.

2020s

New Season

Despite global challenges, A.E.M.I continued to grow in faith and impact. New ministry programmes, building projects, and outreaches were launched.

Today

The Best is Ahead

With 1000+ members, 20+ cell groups, and a growing international network, A.E.M.I moves forward in faith to fulfil its God-given mandate.
