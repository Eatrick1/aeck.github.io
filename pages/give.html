Why We Give

Your generosity enables A.E.M.I to fulfil its God-given mandate of spreading the Gospel, equipping believers, and serving the community.

Missions & Outreach

Your giving sends missionaries across Uganda and beyond, planting churches and sharing the Good News in unreached communities.

Discipleship Programs

We run Bible schools, cell groups, and discipleship programmes that equip believers to grow in their faith and serve effectively.

Building & Infrastructure

We are building a modern worship and ministry centre that will serve the congregation and community for generations to come.

Community Welfare

Our welfare fund supports vulnerable families, widows, orphans, and those in need within our congregation and the broader community.
