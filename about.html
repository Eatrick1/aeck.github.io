Our Story

From a Small Kitchen
to Kampala's Finest Table

Golden Hills Restaurant was founded in 2009 by the Ssekandi family with a clear vision: to create a place where Ugandan warmth meets world-class cuisine. What started as a modest 20-seat dining room on Kampala Road has grown into a 150-seat landmark restaurant that has welcomed over 12,000 guests from Uganda and across the globe.

Our journey has been shaped by a deep respect for local farmers, a love of bold flavours, and the unwavering belief that every guest deserves to feel like family. Every recipe on our menu carries a story — some rooted in generations-old Ugandan tradition, others inspired by the world travels of our culinary team.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve exceptionally crafted food made from the freshest local ingredients, delivered with genuine warmth and hospitality. We exist to create memorable moments — one plate at a time.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's most celebrated restaurant brand — recognized not just for the quality of our food, but for the joy we bring to every table and every community we serve.

The Team

Meet Our Head Chef

Head Chef

Chef Emmanuel Okello

Executive Chef & Co-Founder

Chef Emmanuel trained at the Kenya Utalii College in Nairobi and completed advanced studies at the Cordon Bleu Academy in Paris. He returned to Uganda in 2008 with a passion to elevate local cuisine on the global stage.

His philosophy is simple: "The best cooking starts at the farm gate." He works directly with local farmers in Wakiso and Mukono to source the freshest seasonal produce every week.

Our Core Values

Integrity
