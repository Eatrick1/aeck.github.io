A.E.M.I Since 1990

Welcome to Ambassadors of Encouragement International Ministries

It is an honour that you choose to visit us. Our purpose is to holistically touch the city of Kampala — Uganda and beyond — with the love of Jesus Christ demonstrated in proclamation, in our homes, and our personal lives.

We believe every person is created by God for a purpose, and we are here to help you discover and fulfill that calling. Whether you are exploring faith for the first time or are a long-time believer, there is a place for you here.

Our Ministries

What We Do

At A.E.M.I, we are committed to serving every stage of life — celebrating milestones, empowering communities, and proclaiming the love of Jesus Christ in tangible ways.

Baptism

We celebrate water baptism as a public declaration of faith and surrender to Jesus Christ. Whether for adults or infants, our baptism services are deeply sacred moments of covenant and community.

Women Empowerment

Our Women's Ministry is a vibrant community that equips, encourages and uplifts women in every area of life — spiritual, emotional, and practical. Through prayer breakfasts, workshops, and fellowship.

Youth Empowerment

We are passionate about raising a generation of young people who are grounded in the Word, empowered by the Spirit, and bold in their mission. Discipleship, leadership training, and mentorship.

Wedding Functions
