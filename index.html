Our Story

A Legacy of Flavour,
Born in Kampala

Golden Hills Restaurant opened its doors in 2009 with a simple promise: to serve food that reminds you of home while surprising you with every bite. What began as a small dining room in the heart of Kampala has grown into one of Uganda's most celebrated culinary destinations.

Our head chef, Chef Emmanuel Okello, trained in Nairobi and Paris before returning to Uganda to champion local ingredients with global technique.
